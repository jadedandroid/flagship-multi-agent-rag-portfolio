DESIGN NOTES

What this proves, and what it does not

Implemented

Queries split into at most three subqueries with a bounded retrieval budget. TF-IDF cosine and BM25-style keyword ranking are combined with reciprocal-rank fusion. Responses use exact retrieved text; the validator checks schema, source membership, and exact quote equality. The critic identifies fixture-tagged conflicts and archived versions and flags instruction-like content.

Not claimed

This is not LangGraph, a generative model, a dense embedding service, pgvector, or a live NYC-data connector. Injection checks use explicit patterns; the authored test corpus cannot establish broad semantic faithfulness. The 27 assertions and browser latency measure this demo only.
